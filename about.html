About us

A small team of mountain lovers who want to share the trails we grew up on.

Our story

Cumbre Trails started in 2018 when three friends began taking their colleagues on weekend hikes. Word spread quickly, and soon strangers were asking to join.

Today we organise more than 300 guided hikes a year, but we still keep our groups small so that every hiker gets personal attention.

Our mission

We believe the mountains are for everyone. Our goal is to help people of all fitness levels discover nature safely and responsibly.

We follow Leave No Trace principles on every tour and donate 5% of our profits to local trail maintenance.
